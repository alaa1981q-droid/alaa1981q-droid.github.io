## نظام البيع بالأقساط (claude.ai artifact, NOT in this repo's live site)
The owner's installment-sales app «نظام البيع بالأقساط» lives ONLY as a claude.ai artifact: https://claude.ai/artifact/Kp9Xeak5NGHpSCvU7AESCk (shared "anyone with the link"; capabilities db{rules: root read/write interact, settings write admin}, user, downloads). Backup of the page source: `.dev/aqsat-artifact.html` (copy of the published version; to change the app: Artifact read the url, edit, republish to the SAME url, then refresh this backup). Data is in the artifact db (collections customers, sales, payments, expenses, income, staff, salaries; doc settings/shop) — read with ArtifactData, never overwrite.
- Admin (Editor) sees everything; Contributors (employees) only customers/sales/payments. Platform docs are FROZEN objects: `mapDocs` copies them (`Object.assign({},d.data(),{id})`) — mutating them broke ids once ("الزبون غير موجود"). Writes are applied locally at once (`upsert`/`dropLocal`).
- Home: search (detailed customer cards only while typing; no names otherwise), buttons 👥 الزبائن (view customers), ⏰ المتأخرون (view late), 💰 القاصة (view cash), dashboards day/month/year (profit tile = profit of money received). Nav: back stack (`goTo/goBack/goHome`), «→ رجوع» + «🏠 الرئيسية».
- Customer page: big orange «💵 دفعة على كل مشترياته بوصل واحد» (only when >1 open sale), per-sale «💵 سجّل دفعة على هذه البيعة», «📅 غيّر عدد الأشهر» (monthsForm), schedule table (schedHTML), sale receipt. Payment form = one amount per open sale; >1 → same `no` + `grp` → one grouped receipt (recGrp/grpCopy).
- Receipts print on one A4: green «نسخة الزبون» + blue «نسخة المحل», cut line (recSheet). Printing: window.print on #printArea, fallback «💾 احفظ للطباعة» = downloads capability saving an .html that auto-prints.
- Late (owner's rule): a customer is late when 30+ days passed since his last payment (or sale date); required = floor(days/30) × monthly per open sale; ANY payment removes him (lateOf/lateList). Late report printable (lateSheet).
- Reports: daily / monthly / yearly only (owner removed weekly). Profit of a period = received money × (price−cost)/price (`realized`); «الربح الصافي» = that − expenses − salaries (daily: no salaries). Monthly: salaries per employee + expenses total; daily: expense details; yearly: totals only. Monthly & yearly also show «📈 الأرباح المفروضة على طول تسديد الزبائن» (profitAll: total, realized so far, remaining, expected per month).
- Cash (القاصة, admin): balance = collected + income − expenses − salaries; tabs المصاريف (today/week/month totals) / الموظفون والرواتب / صندوق الوارد / المقبوض من الزبائن; month picker.
- Design: colourful 3D buttons (nav colours: customers blue, sale green, payment orange, cash gold, reports purple, settings teal).
- v21 (owner approved all 10 suggestions): 💬 WhatsApp reminders (`waBtn/waNum` → wa.me/964…, `lateMsg`, `dueMsg`) on late cards, due cards and the customer page; 🤝 guarantor per sale (gName/gPhone/gRel/gAddr, `guarHTML`, collapsible `.gbox` in the sale form; shown on late list/print); 📝 contract print (`contractSheet`: parties, sale table, 6 clauses written by Claude — OWNER SHOULD REVIEW, 3-per-row schedule, signatures seller/buyer/guarantor; from sale card «📝 العقد» and the new-sale receipt); 🪪 ID photos in collection `docs` {customerId,label,img dataURL ≤230KB via `shrink`}, loaded per customer with where().get() (`loadDocs/docsHTML`); 📅 due this week (`nextDue/dueList`, view `due`); 📄 customer statement (`stmtSheet`); 📦 stock (collection `stock` {name,cost,price,qty}, view `stock`, sale form «من المخزن» → `stockId`, qty −1 on sale, +1 on sale delete, `stockMove`); 👤 collector on payments (`col/colName`, select remembered in localStorage aq_col; daily report «الأقساط حسب المستلم»); ⚡ early payoff (payment with `disc` + `payoff:1`; `sumPays` counts amount+disc as credit, `info.paid` subtracts `discOf`, `realOf(p,s)` = (amount+disc)·ratio − disc); 📊 Excel backup in settings (`exportXLSX`, SheetJS 0.18.5 from cdnjs loaded on click, 8 sheets, saved via downloads).
